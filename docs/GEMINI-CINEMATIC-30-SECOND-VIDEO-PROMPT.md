# Gemini Notebook: 30-second Safe Link cinematic video

## Settings

- Studio output: **Video Overview**
- Format: **Cinematic**
- Language: **English**
- Sources: select the Safe Link presentation, strategy and prototype sources

## Steering prompt

```text
Create a premium, photorealistic 30-second cinematic concept film for "Safe Link by SB Security."

PURPOSE
Explain the overall app offering emotionally and clearly, not as a technical explainer. The audience should understand that Safe Link extends SB's trusted home-security relationship to the person and family wherever they go.

STORY AND TIMING
0-4 sec: Dusk in a recognisably South African Johannesburg suburb. A diverse real family leaves a secure home. Warm interior light contrasts with cool evening blue. A subtle navy-and-gold graphic line links the house to a phone.

4-9 sec: Natural close-ups of the Safe Link app based on the supplied prototype: home alarm status, family map and protection readiness. Show a parent checking the home and receiving a simple loved-one arrival confirmation.

9-14 sec: A woman starts a Safe Journey before leaving an appointment. Show calm, purposeful everyday protection, not fear or surveillance.

14-19 sec: A user presses one clear SOS button. Avoid depicting violence or a crime. Show a clean "Signal received" confirmation and precise location moving securely to the SB control room.

19-25 sec: Cut fluidly to a professional, realistic SB control-room operator acknowledging the incident, then an SB response vehicle moving through real suburban streets. Overlay only clear, minimal states: "Acknowledged", "Responder en route", and an ETA without inventing a specific response time.

25-30 sec: Calm resolution. The user sees visible responder progress while family members know help is active. Finish on a dark navy end frame with restrained warm-gold accents:
"Safe Link by SB Security"
"Your home-security relationship, everywhere."

VOICEOVER
Use this exact 65-word script in a warm, calm, authoritative South African English voice:

"Security shouldn't stop at your front gate. Safe Link connects your home, your family, and SB Security wherever life takes you. Check your alarm. Start a safe journey. Know your loved ones have arrived. And when help is needed, one SOS connects you to the SB control room, with visible acknowledgement, responder progress, and clear follow-up. Safe Link by SB Security. Your home-security relationship, everywhere."

VISUAL DIRECTION
Make it feel like a high-end South African financial-services or automotive commercial: cinematic naturalism, authentic people, subtle emotion, 35mm anamorphic character, shallow depth of field, controlled camera movement, physically accurate lighting and reflections, realistic skin, refined contrast and light film grain. Use SB's dark navy, white and warm-gold presentation palette in interface graphics and transitions. Add very subtle blueprint-line motifs from the source deck, but keep the real world dominant.

AUDIO
Use a restrained cinematic pulse that builds gently, subtle phone haptics, quiet control-room ambience and realistic street sound. The voice must remain clear. Avoid loud sirens, horror tension or melodrama.

GUARDRAILS
Use only the supplied Safe Link concept and prototype. Do not invent app screens, customer statistics, response times, national coverage, police integration or guaranteed outcomes. Do not show weapons, attacks, injury, silent audio recording, facial recognition or covert tracking. Do not portray BLE or NFC as live GPS. Do not use American police, 911 references, foreign streets, sci-fi holograms, generic hacker imagery, glowing shields, robotic voices or garbled interface text. Do not invent an SB logo; use only supplied brand assets. Keep all on-screen wording minimal and correctly spelled.

The final result must be one coherent 30-second brand film, not narrated presentation slides, a feature checklist or a montage of unrelated AI shots.
```
