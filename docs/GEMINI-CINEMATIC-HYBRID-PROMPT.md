# Safe Link cinematic hybrid prompt

Use **Video Overview -> Cinematic -> English** and select the Safe Link presentation, strategy and prototype sources.

```text
Create a photorealistic 30-second brand film for "Safe Link by SB Security." It must feel like a premium South African automotive or financial-services commercial, not a presentation, feature list or narrated slideshow. Explain the offering through one connected human story with brief, elegant app close-ups.

CORE IDEA
Safe Link extends SB's trusted home-security relationship beyond the front gate, connecting Home, Person, Family and Response: alarm awareness, family reassurance, Safe Journeys, one-action SOS and visible control-room response.

STORY
Open at dusk in a recognisably Johannesburg suburb. A real, diverse family leaves a secure home. A parent checks Safe Link: the alarm is active and protection readiness is green.

Move naturally into daily life. Show a loved-one arrival confirmation and a woman starting a Safe Journey before leaving an appointment. Use smooth visual links between home, phone and family map. Protection feels calm and present, never intrusive.

When help is needed, show one decisive SOS press without depicting a crime. The app confirms "Signal received." Match-cut to a professional SB control-room operator acknowledging the alert, then an SB response vehicle moving through authentic suburban streets. Show only: "Acknowledged", "Responder en route", "Help active."

End with reassurance, then a dark navy end frame:
"Safe Link by SB Security"
"Your home-security relationship, everywhere."

VOICEOVER
Warm, calm, authoritative South African English:

"Security shouldn't stop at your front gate. Safe Link connects your home, your family and SB Security wherever life takes you. Check your alarm. Know your loved ones are safe. Start a protected journey. And when help is needed, one SOS connects you to the SB control room, with clear acknowledgement and visible response. Safe Link by SB Security. Your home-security relationship, everywhere."

STYLE
Use authentic South African people and locations, cinematic natural light, 35mm lens character, shallow depth of field, smooth match-cuts, realistic skin, subtle film grain and restrained navy-and-gold graphics matching the deck. Add quiet haptics, natural ambience and a confident score that builds gently. Keep text sparse and correctly spelled.

Prioritise cinematic emotion and continuity over showing every feature. No generic stock montage, sci-fi graphics, siren drama, weapons, attacks, American emergency imagery or invented screens, response times, coverage or outcomes. Use only supplied SB/Safe Link material.
```
